## Hypotheses
H0: model-on-model corrections provide no benefit over an honestly calibrated, compute-matched deterministic inverse solver. H1: a trained parameter residual improves target physical measures while preserving untouched measurements. H2: learned local surface/tissue corrections improve independent physical and visual defects without invalid geometry. H3: blinded visual-language guidance improves over equally budgeted numeric/heuristic and shuffled-guidance controls. H4: operator composition preserves declared invariants and reports order effects, rather than silently assuming commuting operations. H5: improvements survive held-out body shape, pose and lighting.

- S2: 24 independently selected, balanced paired native cases. Multi-view and temporal ground truth; compare learned regional residual and blind VLM-guided proposals against constrained numeric and shuffled controls under identical generation/render budgets.

- S4: only when S3 demands it, LoRA/task-vector/activation/hypernetwork experiments with exact shared-base/layer/rights admission, independent weight and numerical stability controls.
